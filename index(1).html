Frequently asked

Answers before your first visit.

If you are unsure whether your child may benefit from speech and language therapy, an initial assessment can help clarify the next step.

Book on Instagram
What services does Awaaz Therapy Centre offer?

We support speech and language delay, autism-related communication needs, ADHD-related communication and interaction, articulation and phonology, stuttering, hearing impairment, voice disorders and aphasia.

At what age should I be concerned about my child’s speech?

If you have concerns about your child’s communication development, an assessment can help identify strengths, needs and appropriate next steps. Development varies between children, so concerns are best considered in the context of the whole child.

How do I know if my child may need speech therapy?

Signs can include difficulty expressing needs, limited vocabulary, unclear speech, difficulty understanding instructions, stuttering, or challenges with social communication. An assessment can help clarify whether therapy is appropriate.

What happens during the first assessment?

We discuss your concerns, observe communication and assess relevant speech and language skills. Findings are then used to guide goals and recommendations.

Can parents be involved in therapy?

Yes. Parent guidance can help communication strategies become part of everyday routines at home.

Do you work with children with Autism and ADHD?

Yes. Support can include functional communication, listening, interaction and social communication goals based on the child’s individual needs.
