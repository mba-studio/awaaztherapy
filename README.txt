AWAAZ THERAPY CENTRE WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html    Main website
- styles.css   Responsive visual design
- script.js    Mobile navigation, scroll reveals, FAQ behavior, assessment email form
- assets/      Logo and Fatima Shah photo supplied by the client

How to use:
1. Upload the entire folder contents to your web hosting public_html / www directory.
2. Keep the assets folder next to index.html.
3. The assessment form works without a server: it opens the visitor's email app with their information pre-filled.

Contact links, Google Maps, Instagram and Facebook are already included.
